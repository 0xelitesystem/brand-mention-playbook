# 04. Monthly review procedure

First weekday of each month. 30 minutes. Sit down with the tracker spreadsheet and run the steps below in order. Skip steps at your peril; each one feeds the next.

## Step 1: Count the totals (5 min)

Open the spreadsheet. Filter for last calendar month. Count:

- Total mentions
- Mentions by source category (news, blog, podcast, forum, social, other)
- Mentions by sentiment (positive, neutral, negative)
- Mentions that linked back to your site
- Unique source domains

Log the counts at the bottom of the spreadsheet in a "Monthly totals" section.

## Step 2: Compare to last month (3 min)

For each metric in step 1, is it up, flat, or down vs the prior month?

- Up: note what changed. Did you publish something, get featured, launch?
- Flat: normal baseline.
- Down: not necessarily bad. Some months are quiet.

Three months of down on every metric is a signal. One month of down is not.

## Step 3: Read the top 5 mentions (10 min)

Sort by author followers + source authority (combination of reach + relevance). Open the top 5 actual URLs. Read what was said.

For each:

- Was the mention accurate?
- Was there an explicit recommendation or just a passing reference?
- Did the author link back to the right page?
- Is this a relationship worth investing in?

Log notes in the "Notes" column.

## Step 4: Action items (5 min)

From the top 5 and any flagged mentions, generate action items:

- Thank-you to send (use `07-outreach-templates.md`)
- Correction to request (only if factually wrong; use `07-outreach-templates.md`)
- Link-back request (only if mention is positive but unlinked)
- Outreach to author (only for high-authority sources where deeper relationship makes sense)

Cap at 5 action items per month. More than 5 means you are not prioritizing.

## Step 5: AI engine test (skip if last test was over 21 days ago)

If your last test in `06-ai-engine-test-template.md` was over 21 days ago, run it now (allocate 45 minutes outside the 30-minute review for this). Otherwise skip.

## Step 6: Flag patterns (5 min)

What kinds of mentions are you getting most? What kinds are you missing?

Examples of useful patterns:

- "Most mentions come from people who use the free tier. Paid tier users barely talk about us in public."
- "Founder name gets more mentions than brand name. We should lean into the personal brand."
- "Three different sources mentioned us in 'best alternatives' lists for Competitor X this month. That competitor is leaking customers."
- "Almost zero mentions in non-English sources. Translation strategy?"

Add a "Patterns this month" note to the spreadsheet. Review the last 6 months of patterns quarterly.

## Step 7: Save and archive

Save the spreadsheet. If using Google Sheets, version history is automatic. If using local Excel, do a "Save As" with month suffix: `brand-mentions-2026-05.xlsx`.

## What this is NOT

- Not a sentiment analysis project. You are not building a dashboard. You are reading actual mentions and acting on them.
- Not a ranking exercise. Numbers are context, not the point.
- Not a daily activity. Daily checking creates noise. Monthly creates signal.

## Failure modes

- Skipping months because "not much happened." Run the review anyway; the absence of mentions is itself data.
- Action item explosion. Capping at 5 forces real prioritization.
- Treating Google Alerts emails as the inbox. They are an input to the spreadsheet, not the review surface itself.

## Yearly rollup

In January, build a 12-month rollup with totals per month, patterns spotted, and biggest wins. This becomes your reference for the next year's planning.
